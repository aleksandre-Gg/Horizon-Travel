Why visit Prague?

Prague is an important city with artistic and musical heritage, its historic center dates back to 11th-14th century.

Its buildings, encompassing a blend of medieval, renaissance and baroque architecture could be compared to an open air history museum.


People usually choose Prague for its historic sites, affordable prices, great food and world famous beer culture.

So if you are interested in either of those things, you will definitely like Prague.


Beyond traditional heritage sites, Prague also offers modern artistic installments and museums you can visit.

The highly renowned novelist Franz Kafka was born in Prague and spent most of his days there. an outdoor kinetic sculpture dedicated to him, made in 2014 by David Černý attracted a lot of people's attention worldwide.


While it is true that Prague can get quite touristy at times, there are several non touristy districts we will recommend to get away from busy areas.

Best time to visit

The best time to visit prague is in September to October, since the crowd is mild and temperatures may drop as low as 0°C in winter.


While packing your essentials, don't forget to bring comfortable walking shoes, winter coat/layers of clothes if you decide to go from Nov-Feb.


We also recommend April to May for pleasant walks in the square and casual strolling.


Please keep in mind that during summer, major tourist spots may get overcrowded frequently. Because of that, we generally avoid booking in July or August.
