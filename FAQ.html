Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my trip?

We recommend reaching out at least 4-6 weeks before your travel dates, especially for custom planned trips. This gives us time to look for the best hotels for availability.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments made with credit and debit cards such as Visa, Mastercard and American Express.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. All payments are processed through secure encrypted channels so your information stays protected.

How do you choose the accommodations you recommend?

We personally search through establishments known for their comfort and location.

We also factor in your preferences and any feedback you share so the final pick actually fits what you're looking for.

Do you help with visas or travel documents?

Yes. We can guide you through what you'll need for your destination and help with the paperwork and documentation.

What happens if there's an emergency during my trip?

Our support team is available 24/7 exactly for this.

Please reach out to us if you're experiencing issues/want to cancel.
