Curated moments from trips

"I arrived in Bangkok carrying only a suitcase and not one plan further than the first night. Horizon Travel booked me a tiny hotel close to Chinatown. Before dark I found myself enjoying a deliciously sweet mango sticky rice from a street vendor who had no name and a long queue.

A few days later I traveled north to Chiang Mai, where the mountain air tasted different. Stumbled into a lantern festival by sheer chance, got hopelessly lost looking for my guesthouse twice and enjoyed both experiences.

Koh Lanta was quiet and sunburned. Days started off with coffee sipping on a wooden dock and afternoons went by without any particular activity.

When I left to go back home, the camera roll on my phone held pictures of food and street dogs. That seemed to sum up my entire trip."

-Allyson M.
