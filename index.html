Thailand • Bangkok

"I arrived in Bangkok with just one suitcase, not knowing where I would be spending the night. Horizon Travel helped me book an affordable hotel room, with breakfast included.

The next day I spent my time walking around, trying to fit in as many sights I could see in my short time period in Bangkok. The provided guide has helped me with my time schedule, I went from the Grand Palace to Wat Pho, took a riverboat across Chao Phraya to Wat Arun, then I went to King Power Mahanakhon to overlook the city. My day ended on Yaowarat Road.

When I was returning home and scrolling through my photos I realized I took so many photos of food, as well as adorable street cats and dogs."

Posted on: 2026-07-28

Ireland • Dublin

"Spent a few days in Dublin with my friends. We love going to bars to drink and chat, and Ireland has a LOT of them. We were checking out different pubs every single afternoon! We had tons of fun just hanging out together.

Despite that, we didn't want to spend the rest our time aimlessly walking around, so we went with a local guide recommended by Horizon Travel. I can't say either of us are keen on using travel agencies, so we were pleasantly surprised.

Our guide covered Dublin Castle, Ha'penny Bridge, The River Liffey and Temple Bar Cultural Quarter. They were really helpful and nice. I have to say, Dublin really has a lot of statues. Here's a pic I took of one of them."

Posted on: 2026-02-12

Canada • Banff

"I'm an experienced solo traveller, I especially love going hiking, it brings my mind peace and quiet.

Although I frequently end up losing track of time jumping to different sources of information while looking things up. I chose Horizon because they provide sufficient services and data on their own. One of the main goals in my life is to travel as much as possible, so the aspect of conserving time is important to me.

This year, I'm really happy with my decision to go to the Canadian Rockies. Most areas around here are tranquil and the nature is beautiful. Hiking trails range from very easy to challenging. I like that I can change the course of my direction whenever I feel like it."

Hong Kong • Hong Kong

"Urban exploring, cityscapes and skylines are some things me and my partner are into.

That's why we decided to plan our trip to Hong Kong. Our specialized travel advisor helped us with navigating visa regulations and arranging private transfers. Our ferry was cancelled due to bad weather, so we had to call in support.

Hong Kong was amazing, it was lively and especially beautiful at night. We had a great time in Kowloon. Our favorite spot was the Nan Lian Garden. Hong Kong Island also has a lot to offer, it has a more modern vibe than Kowloon. The food at local stalls and markets is delicious, we frequently visited them. Overall our trip couldn't have been better."

Posted on: 2026-08-21
