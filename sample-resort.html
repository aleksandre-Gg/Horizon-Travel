About this resort

Relaxation, wellness, multi-course meals and rooms built for your comfort. Isla Palms was built along the coastline of Riviera Nayarit, spanning the beachfront with stunning views of the pacific ocean. Staying with us is a perfect way to unwind, if waking up to peaceful sounds of the ocean waves is something you look forward to.

Our amenities include a full-service spa with hydrotherapy for stress relief, outside pool, alongside a modern fitness center. We also offer meals made with fresh, organic and local ingredients for fine dining.

Nearest establishments within the area, such as a restaurant, a bar or a grocery store are just a few minutes of walk away. Our guests especially enjoy walks along the beach, while watching the sun set over the ocean in the horizon.

We're looking forward to your visit with us!

Property policies

ID/Passport

Valid ID or passport is required for every guest at check-in.

Check-in time

Check-in starts at 3:00 PM, check-out is by 11:00 AM. Late check-out is available until 1:00 PM, with an additional fee.

Extra beds

Extra beds available for each suite upon request.

Accessibility

Accessible bathrooms, entries and paths for wheelchair users.

Pets

Pets and service animals are welcome. We request that pets remain supervised at all times for safety.

Smoking

Smoking indoors is strictly prohibited.

Payment

All major credit and debit cards are accepted.
