About us

Horizon Travel is a travel agency aiming to simplify travel.

We combine local knowledge and efficient scheduling

to make your stay enjoyable.

Our Story

Horizon Travel was founded by a group of enthusiastic travelers who wanted to share their passion and experience with other explorers, in order to uncover the best in each city together.

Starting off small with only a handful of people, we've grown into an agency devoted to providing the best travel packages to our customers, having in mind that travel should be uncomplicated and less time-consuming.

We've travelled to countless cities in order to find out the best routes, hotels and sites to visit for first-time travellers or for people who don't want to go through the hassle of planning out each step in a foreign country.

Whatever plans you have for a getaway our trips are designed specifically for you.

Arranging the perfect journey is the very purpose behind Horizon Travel.

Our mission

Making travel accessible for everyone is our priority.

At Horizon Travel, we plan and organize tours and travel packages tailored to your tastes. We know firsthand how expensive travel can be. Providing affordable services is a key focus of ours, so a great trip doesn't have to come with a big price tag.

What do you get by booking your trip with Horizon?

Your time and your energy goes into enjoying the vacation, instead of worrying about unnecessary expenses. we purposely look into and choose the best available suites for our customers to ensure a positive impression while traveling abroad.

Every year, we work towards expanding our reach.

Which involves new destinations and routes organized by the team, as well as growing connections with partners.

Each new addition is chosen with the same care as our first, a wider reach only matters if it still feels deliberate and thought out.

Because our goal of turning your dream vacations into real experiences stays the same.

So we continue to listening to the travelers, to the people who call these places home. This determines our path moving forward as a team.
